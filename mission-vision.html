Our Calling

Our Mission

“To spread the Good News of Salvation and the truths of the Bible, and bring lost souls to the Lord Jesus Christ — the Giver of Hope.”

Our Foundation

Our church was established to give honor and glory to God, to make disciples of the Lord Jesus Christ, and to build up men and women for the ministry and the proclamation of the full gospel message in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Goal

To make disciples of the Lord Jesus Christ — believers who live as their Master lives. We aim not only to reach people, but to raise disciples who disciple others and participate in expanding His Kingdom to every people group.

One Message & Method

One Message: The full gospel of His Kingdom — not only deliverance from sin, but the abundant life God desires for His people today.
Our Method: Declaration and demonstration in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Direction

Our Vision

“To be a Christ-centered, Spirit-empowered, and Bible-based church that ministers to the whole family and reaches the world with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.”

We envision building a church with a strong foundation in God’s Word, a passion for winning souls, and a commitment to disciple believers who will impact society with Kingdom values.

Our vision is to see transformed lives, strong families, vibrant communities, and nations touched by the power of God through faithful discipleship and mission.
